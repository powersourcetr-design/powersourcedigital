The question is usually asked as "which is better", and the honest answer is that they solve different problems. Ads buy visibility now. SEO builds an asset that produces visibility later at no cost per visit.

So the decision is not about which is better in the abstract, but about which fits your situation. Our search work is on the [SEO](route:service/seo) page.

## The difference that decides most cases

Ads are **rent**. You pay, you appear. You stop paying, you disappear the same day. Nothing accumulates.

SEO is **ownership**. You pay once for the work, and a page that reaches a good position keeps bringing visitors. But it is slow, and nobody can guarantee you a position.

## When ads are the right call

- **A deadline** — a seasonal offer or a launch that cannot wait six months
- **Testing demand** — you want to know whether people buy this at all before investing in content
- **A new product** with no ranking page yet
- **High margins** that absorb a cost per click comfortably

Ads are also an excellent research tool. Within two weeks they tell you which terms produce actual enquiries rather than just traffic, and that finding directs SEO work precisely instead of by guesswork.

## When SEO is the right call

- **Steady demand** for what you sell, year-round
- **Thin margins** that cannot carry a repeated click cost
- **A local business** that can appear on Maps, which is the cheapest channel available — see [getting found on Google Maps in Riyadh](route:blogPost/google-business-profile-riyadh)
- **A time horizon** that allows three to six months before results

## When neither works

If your site does not convert, both are spending in the wrong place. Ads bring visitors who leave. SEO brings visitors who leave for free. The result is the same: no enquiries.

Here is the test. Look at how many visitors you had last month and how many enquiries came out. If that ratio is very poor, the problem is the pages, not the traffic source — and [what a website costs](route:blogPost/website-design-cost-saudi-arabia) is the right place to start.

## The order we usually recommend

**First**, the Google Business Profile. Free, and the fastest source of enquiries for any business with a physical location.

**Second**, your service and product pages. These serve both channels, because an ad landing on a weak page wastes its budget.

**Third**, a focused ad campaign on two or three high-intent terms, to learn what actually sells.

**Fourth**, SEO built on what the ads taught you.

The order is deliberate: each step makes the next one cheaper.

## The short version

If you need enquiries this month, advertise. If you are building a business over years, invest in search. If your budget allows only one and your business is local, start with the profile — [local SEO in Riyadh](route:blogPost/local-seo-riyadh) covers the rest.
