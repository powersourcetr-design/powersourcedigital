There is no best platform, only a best fit, and for most Saudi stores the honest answer is **Salla or Zid unless you are selling across borders**. That is an unpopular position among agencies who resell one platform, which is a good reason to be careful whose comparison you read.

## Where each one wins

**Salla** has the largest merchant base in the Kingdom and the widest local app selection of the three. If you want the safest local default, it is usually this one.

**Zid** is close on features and worth quoting against Salla rather than dismissing. Onboarding tends to be simpler, and depending on your order volume the pricing can work out lower.

**Shopify** earns its cost when you sell internationally, need a specific integration from its app store, or want a theme built exactly to a design. Its ecosystem is not close to being matched locally.

## The costs that catch people out

The subscription is rarely the real number. Budget also for:

- **Payment gateway fees** on every order — a percentage, not a fixed cost, so it scales with your success
- **Apps**, on Shopify especially, where four or five at 10 to 30 dollars each quietly doubles the monthly bill
- **A theme**, if you want something beyond the free options
- **Shipping integration**, depending on carrier

Full figures are in [what an online store costs](route:blogPost/ecommerce-website-cost-saudi-arabia).

## ZATCA is not a detail

Every store taking money in Saudi Arabia has e-invoicing obligations, and this is the one area where the local platforms are meaningfully ahead: it is native rather than bolted on. On Shopify it works through an app — one more thing to configure and keep working. [What ZATCA requires of an online store](route:blogPost/zatca-e-invoicing-online-stores) covers the detail.

## Migrating later

Moving is possible and routinely underestimated. The catalogue exports cleanly. What breaks is your URLs: every product and category address changes, and unless each old one redirects to its new equivalent, you lose search rankings you spent years earning. The work is not difficult, but it has to be planned before the switch rather than discovered after traffic falls.

## How to actually decide

Answer one question: **will a meaningful share of your orders come from outside Saudi Arabia within two years?**

If yes, Shopify. If no, Salla or Zid — quote both and choose on cost and on which admin your team prefers, because they will be in it every day.
